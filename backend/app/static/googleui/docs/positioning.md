# AIR4 — Positioning

## What is AIR4
AIR4 is an external brain.
It reflects your thinking, stores context locally, and allows you to
continue decisions and reasoning without loss.

AIR4 runs entirely on your device.
No cloud. No data leaks. No dependency.

## Meaning
AIR4 — Artificial Intellect Reflection for Freedom.

Reflection — AIR4 mirrors and extends your thinking.
Freedom — you control your data, memory, and cognition.

## Core Scenarios
1. Think  
   A space for reasoning and decisions. Dialog is a tool, not the goal.

2. Store  
   Documents, notes, and knowledge become long-term memory.

3. Recall  
   Return to past context and continue thinking where you left off.

## What AIR4 is not
- Not a chat-first assistant
- Not a cloud service
- Not a mass-market SaaS
- Not an automation-first agent

AIR4 exists to reduce cognitive load and preserve context.
